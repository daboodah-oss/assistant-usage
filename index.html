Subscription usage across Jeff's AI assistants · last updated Oct 30, 2026 05:45 AM EDT

% of own limit — all assistants

Each assistant samples its own platform's subscription every ~15 minutes. Percentages are % of that platform's own limit, so the lines are comparable as "how hot each subscription is running". Burn rate is a least-squares fit over each lane's headline series; dashed lines project it to the reset (capped at 100%). The Muse card's free-tier equivalence uses the estimated 5x Power/Free allowance ratio, and the "Free" tick on its bar marks where one full Free-tier week runs out (20% of the Power bar). One repo, one lane per assistant: daboodah-oss/assistant-usage.
