Each assistant samples its own platform's subscription every ~15 minutes. Percentages are % of that platform's own limit, so the lines are comparable as "how hot each subscription is running". Burn rate is a least-squares fit over each lane's headline series; dashed lines project it to the reset (capped at 100%).
